Company overview

We started Eleven Energy in 2023 to give Nigerian businesses and communities energy they can count on, that costs less to run and does less harm. Our team brings years of experience in renewable technology, engineering and project management, and we keep testing what’s new so every system we build runs better than the last.

Our mission

Sustainable energy for every sector, from homes to commercial hubs and industrial centres.

We help clients use energy better, spend less on running their sites and make them more sustainable. A household, a growing small business or a factory floor all need the same thing from us: reliable, efficient power that sets them up for a greener, more productive future.

We want to be the provider that communities, businesses and small enterprises trust first.

Our vision

To be Nigeria’s leading provider of renewable energy systems.

We want to raise the standard for energy in every sector, and change how industries and communities use and manage power, with systems that adapt to businesses and communities of every size and type.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to.

Four values shape how we design, how we install and how we treat the people we work for.

  1. 01

    Innovation

    We take on new technology and fresh approaches when they make a system work better, and push renewable energy further than the standard package.

  2. 02

    Customer focus

    We build long relationships with the people we work for. Every design starts with understanding what their site actually needs.

  3. 03

    Environmental and community impact

    We replace the noise, fumes and cost of diesel with cleaner power that communities can live alongside.

  4. 04

    Integrity

    We are honest about what a system will and won’t do, open about what it costs, and ethical in how we work.
