Understand the calculations
  • Daily use is watts × quantity × hours × duty cycle, converted to kWh.
  • Solar capacity divides daily use by peak sun hours and system efficiency, then rounds up to whole panels.
  • The inverter estimate applies the simultaneous load percentage to connected power.
  • The battery estimate covers selected backup loads for the lesser of backup hours and daily run time, adjusted for usable battery capacity.
